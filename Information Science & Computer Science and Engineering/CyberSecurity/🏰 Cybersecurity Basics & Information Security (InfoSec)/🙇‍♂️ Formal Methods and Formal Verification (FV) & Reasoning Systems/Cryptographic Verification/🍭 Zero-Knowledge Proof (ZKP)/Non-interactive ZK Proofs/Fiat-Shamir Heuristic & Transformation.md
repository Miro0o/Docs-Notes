## Intro
> 🔗 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat%E2%80%93Shamir_heuristic

In [cryptography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptography "Cryptography"), the **Fiat–Shamir heuristic**, or **Fiat–Shamir transformation**, is a technique for taking an interactive [proof of knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_knowledge "Proof of knowledge") and creating a [digital signature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_signature "Digital signature") based on it. This way, some fact (for example, knowledge of a certain secret number) can be publicly proven without revealing underlying information. The technique is due to [Amos Fiat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_Fiat "Amos Fiat") and [Adi Shamir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adi_Shamir "Adi Shamir") (1986). For the method to work, the original interactive proof must have the property of being [public-coin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interactive_proof_system#Public_coin_protocol_versus_private_coin_protocol "Interactive proof system"), i.e. verifier's random coins are made public throughout the proof protocol.
