> [!tip] Reading the ML conference summaries
> Counts below use the **main paper track**. Separate datasets/benchmarks, position, competition, workshop, and journal-to-conference tracks are stated separately rather than mixed into the headline rate. All three conferences publish thousands of papers and normally give several Outstanding/Best Paper awards. A practical first pass is: award papers → oral papers → spotlight papers → posters in one topic. “Oral,” “spotlight,” and “poster” are **presentation distinctions**, not separate publication tiers: all accepted main papers enter the proceedings.
>
> **Format at a glance:** ICLR, ICML, and NeurIPS give every accepted main paper a poster; small subsets receive oral or spotlight presentation distinctions. Their broader week includes invited talks, tutorials, workshops, competitions/challenges, affinity events, and an expo. ICML 2026 made physical presentation optional and replaced the old oral/spotlight attendance requirement with presentation distinctions.

> [!tip] Reading the NLP conference summaries
> Since 2024, most papers first pass through **ACL Rolling Review (ARR)** and are then *committed* to a conference. Consequently, official reports may use an upstream ARR pool, a venue-preference pool, or only committed papers as the denominator. Each entry states the denominator rather than treating the rates as directly interchangeable. “Main” and **Findings** are separate publication outcomes; long and short papers can enter either. Best-paper pages often contain several Best, Outstanding, Theme, Resource, and Student Paper awards.
>
> **Format at a glance:** main papers are assigned oral or poster presentations; Findings papers are predominantly posters, with some selected talks depending on the year. The conference week also includes tutorials, workshops, system demonstrations, industry and student-research tracks, shared tasks, journal-to-conference talks, and virtual poster components.

一般认为 CVPR、ICCV、ECCV 为 CV 三大顶会。

> [!tip] Reading the vision conference summaries
> CVPR is annual; **ICCV runs in odd years** and **ECCV in even years**, so “no edition” is expected rather than missing data. The headline counts use the main paper track. Every accepted paper normally has a poster; only a small subset receives an oral presentation, and some editions also label “highlight” papers. Best Paper, Best Student Paper, and honorable mentions are separate awards—use the linked complete award lists rather than treating one title as the only frontier signal.
>
> **Format at a glance:** all three combine main-conference oral/poster sessions with tutorials, workshops, challenges, demos, an expo/industry program, and doctoral/student activities. Oral/highlight labels are presentation distinctions; accepted poster papers are full proceedings papers too.
