The ACM Symposium on Operating Systems Principles (SOSP) is a conference that brings together developers and researchers from academia and industry to advance the science and technology in operating systems. The conference is sponsored by the [ACM Special Interest Group on Operating Systems (SIGOPS)](http://www.acm.org/sigops). It has been held once every two years since 1967, when the first SOSP conference took place in Gatlinburg, Tennessee. Information on SOSP conferences is provided below.

## Conferences

> [!tip] Reading these systems conference summaries
> Counts are for reviewable main-track submissions. OSDI used **revise-and-resubmit carry-ins** in 2023–2025, so the number printed in the program can exceed the number newly accepted from that year's submissions; both values are recorded. SOSP was historically biennial but has been **annual since 2023**. Both venues emphasize complete implemented systems and normally present every accepted paper orally. Start with the Jay Lepreau/SOSP best-paper winners, then read one session end-to-end to compare how papers frame system design, evaluation, and operational trade-offs.
>
> **Format at a glance:** OSDI was single-track through 2025 and moved to its first **three-track** program in 2026 after a large expansion; SOSP remains centered on plenary/single-track paper talks. Both add keynotes, posters (including early-stage work), artifact evaluation, mentoring/community events, and workshops or co-located events.

### OSDI

🏠 [Series home](https://www.usenix.org/conferences/byname/179)

#### 2026

- **Conference:** [OSDI 2026, July 13–15, Seattle](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26)
- **Submissions → accepted:** **669 reviewable → 134 new acceptances (20.0%)**, plus **2** revise-and-resubmit papers carried from OSDI 2025, for **136 program papers**. Nineteen are designated Operational Systems papers ([official chairs' message](https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/osdi26-message.pdf)).
- **Award:** Jay Lepreau Best Paper Award winner(s) are identified in the [official technical program](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi26/technical-sessions).
- **Format:** the first multi-track OSDI—three parallel oral tracks—plus a separately selected poster session, keynote, artifact evaluation, and mentoring activities.

#### 2025

- **Conference:** [OSDI 2025, July 7–9, Boston](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi25)
- **Submissions → accepted:** **339 → 48 new acceptances (14.2%)**, plus **5** OSDI 2024 revise-and-resubmit papers, for **53 program papers (15.6% when divided by current submissions)** ([official chairs' message](https://www.usenix.org/sites/default/files/osdi25-message.pdf)).
- **Award:** Jay Lepreau Best Paper Award winner(s) are marked in the [technical program](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi25/technical-sessions).
- **Format:** single-track oral paper talks, keynote, posters/reception, artifact evaluation, and co-location with USENIX ATC.

#### 2024

- **Conference:** [OSDI 2024, July 10–12, Santa Clara](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi24)
- **Submissions → accepted:** **272 → 49 new acceptances (18.0%)**, plus **4** OSDI 2023 revise-and-resubmit papers, for **53 program papers** ([official proceedings front matter](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi24_full_proceedings.pdf)).
- **Award:** Jay Lepreau Best Paper Award winner(s) are marked in the [technical program](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi24/technical-sessions).
- **Format:** single-track oral talks, keynote, **52 accepted posters**, artifact evaluation, and co-location with USENIX ATC.

#### 2023

- **Conference:** [OSDI 2023, July 10–12, Boston](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi23)
- **Submissions → accepted:** **255 → 50 current-cycle acceptances (19.6%)**, with **4** earlier revise-and-resubmit papers producing a **54-paper program** ([official proceedings](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi23_full_proceedings.pdf); the public submission site reports the 50/255 current pool).
- **Award:** Jay Lepreau Best Paper Award winner(s) are marked in the [technical program](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi23/technical-sessions).
- **Format:** single-track oral talks, keynote, **33 accepted posters**, artifact evaluation, and co-location with USENIX ATC.
