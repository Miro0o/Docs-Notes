## Conferences

> [!tip] Reading these software-engineering conference summaries
> Counts are for the **main research track** only; industry, NIER/new-ideas, journal-first, demos/tools, datasets, doctoral symposium, and workshop papers have separate review pools. Since 2024–2026 several venues use dual cycles or major revision, so “submitted” means papers entering those research cycles. The usual paper honor is a set of **ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards**, not a single best paper; award badges on the linked official programs give the complete list.
>
> **Format at a glance:** main research papers are presented orally in parallel sessions. The broader conference programs add keynotes, journal-first talks, industry tracks, posters and ACM Student Research Competition, tool/demo and dataset tracks, artifact evaluation/badges, doctoral symposia, tutorials, and workshops. ISSTA focuses most tightly on testing and program analysis; ASE on automation; FSE on foundations and methods; ICSE spans the full field.
