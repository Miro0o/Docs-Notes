## Conferences

> [!tip] Reading these PL conference summaries
> Counts below refer to the main research-paper/PACMPL track, not workshops, SRC abstracts, journal-first talks, or invited papers. PLDI and POPL publish conventional annual proceedings (POPL through PACMPL); OOPSLA is a **PACMPL journal issue presented at SPLASH**, with two submission rounds and revision decisions, so its acceptance rate is not directly comparable. These venues normally give several **ACM SIGPLAN Distinguished Paper Awards**, not one best paper. Start with the award-badged papers, then use the program sessions to follow one theme—compilers, verification, semantics/types, synthesis, runtimes, or language design—across years.
>
> **Format at a glance:** accepted research papers receive oral presentations; PLDI/POPL week and SPLASH also contain workshops, tutorials, keynotes, artifact evaluation, and an ACM Student Research Competition whose first stage is a poster session. OOPSLA papers are published in the year's PACMPL issue and presented at SPLASH, whose companion program also includes posters, demos, a doctoral symposium, and Onward!.
