## How to Use Surveys

- use them to discover vocabulary, datasets, and earlier work;
- verify venue, year, and technical claims against the primary paper;
- inspect inclusion dates because the field changes faster than review cycles;
- separate code-model training, LLM-for-software, and software-for-LLM work;
- check whether “agent” means a genuine environment loop or prompt chaining;
- avoid importing security-first work into this dossier through an overly broad survey taxonomy.
