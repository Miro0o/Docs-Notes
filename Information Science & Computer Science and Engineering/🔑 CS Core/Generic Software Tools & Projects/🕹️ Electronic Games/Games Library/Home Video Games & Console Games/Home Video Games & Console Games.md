## Intro
> 🔗 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game#

A **video game**, **computer game**, or simply **game** is an [electronic game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_game "Electronic game") that involves interaction with a [user interface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/User_interface "User interface") or [input device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Input_device "Input device") (such as a [joystick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joystick "Joystick"), [controller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_controller "Game controller"), [keyboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_keyboard "Computer keyboard"), or [motion sensing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_sensing "Motion sensing") device) to generate [visual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visual "Visual") [feedback](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feedback "Feedback") from a [display device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Display_device "Display device"), most commonly shown in a [video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video "Video") format on a [television set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_set "Television set"), [computer monitor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_monitor "Computer monitor"), [flat-panel display](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flat-panel_display "Flat-panel display") or [touchscreen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touchscreen "Touchscreen") on [handheld devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handheld_device "Handheld device"), or a [virtual reality headset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_reality_headset "Virtual reality headset"). Most modern video games are [audiovisual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audiovisual "Audiovisual"), with [audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound "Sound") complement delivered through [speakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loudspeaker "Loudspeaker") or [headphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Headphones "Headphones"), and sometimes also with other types of sensory feedback (e.g., [haptic technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haptic_technology "Haptic technology") that provides [tactile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Touch "Touch") sensations). Some video games also allow [microphone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microphone "Microphone") and [webcam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Webcam "Webcam") inputs for [in-game chatting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_chat_in_online_gaming "Voice chat in online gaming") and [livestreaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_livestreaming "Video game livestreaming").

Video games are typically categorized according to their [hardware platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardware_platform "Hardware platform"), which traditionally includes [arcade video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcade_video_game "Arcade video game"), [console games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Console_game "Console game"), and [computer games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PC_game "PC game") (which includes [LAN games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_game "Network game"), [online games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_game "Online game"), and [browser games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Browser_game "Browser game")). More recently, the [video game industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_industry "Video game industry") has expanded onto [mobile gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_gaming "Mobile gaming") through [mobile devices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_device "Mobile device") (such as [smartphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphones "Smartphones") and [tablet computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tablet_computers "Tablet computers")), [virtual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_reality "Virtual reality") and [augmented reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augmented_reality "Augmented reality") systems, and remote [cloud gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_gaming "Cloud gaming"). Video games are also classified into a wide range of [genres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_genre "Video game genre") based on their style of [gameplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gameplay "Gameplay") and [target audience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Target_audience "Target audience").

The first video game prototypes in the 1950s and 1960s were simple extensions of electronic games using video-like output from large, room-sized [mainframe computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainframe_computers "Mainframe computers"). The first consumer video game was the arcade video game _[Computer Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Space "Computer Space")_ in 1971, which took inspiration from the earlier 1962 computer game _[Spacewar!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spacewar! "Spacewar!")_. In 1972 came the now-iconic video game _[Pong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pong "Pong")_ and the first [home console](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_video_game_console "Home video game console"), the [Magnavox Odyssey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnavox_Odyssey "Magnavox Odyssey"). The industry grew quickly during the ["golden age" of arcade video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_age_of_arcade_video_games "Golden age of arcade video games") from the late 1970s to early 1980s but suffered from the [crash of the North American video game market in 1983](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1983_video_game_crash "1983 video game crash") due to loss of publishing control and saturation of the market. Following the crash, the industry matured, was dominated by Japanese companies such as [Nintendo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo "Nintendo"), [Sega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sega "Sega"), and [Sony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Interactive_Entertainment "Sony Interactive Entertainment"), and established practices and methods around the development and distribution of video games to prevent a similar crash in the future, many of which continue to be followed. In the 2000s, the core industry centered on "[AAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAA_\(video_game_industry\) "AAA (video game industry)")" games, leaving little room for riskier experimental games. Coupled with the availability of the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet "Internet") and [digital distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_distribution_of_video_games "Digital distribution of video games"), this gave room for [independent video game development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_video_game_development "Independent video game development") (or "[indie games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indie_game "Indie game")") to gain prominence into the 2010s. Since then, the commercial importance of the [video game industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_industry "Video game industry") has been increasing. The emerging Asian markets and proliferation of smartphone games in particular are altering player demographics towards [casual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casual_games "Casual games") and [cozy gaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cozy_game "Cozy game"), and increasing [monetization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_monetization "Video game monetization") by incorporating [games as a service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Games_as_a_service "Games as a service").

Today, [video game development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_development "Video game development") requires numerous skills, [vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foresight_\(psychology\) "Foresight (psychology)"), [teamwork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teamwork "Teamwork"), and [liaisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaboration "Collaboration") between different parties, including [developers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_developers "Video game developers"), [publishers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_publishers "Video game publishers"), [distributors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_\(marketing\) "Distribution (marketing)"), [retailers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retailer "Retailer"), [hardware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_hardware "Electronic hardware") manufacturers, and other marketers, to successfully bring a game to its consumers. As of 2020, the global video game market had estimated annual revenues of US$159 billion across hardware, software, and services, which is three times the size of the global [music industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_industry "Music industry") and four times that of the [film industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_industry "Film industry") in 2019, making it a formidable heavyweight across the modern [entertainment industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_industry "Entertainment industry"). The video game market is also a major influence behind the [electronics industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronics_industry "Electronics industry"), where [personal computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_computer "Personal computer") component, console, and peripheral sales, as well as consumer demands for better game performance, have been powerful driving factors for hardware design and innovation.
