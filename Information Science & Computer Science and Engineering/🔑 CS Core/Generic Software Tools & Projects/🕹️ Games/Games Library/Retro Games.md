## Intro
### Retro-gaming
> 🔗 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retro_gaming

**Retro gaming**, also known as **classic gaming** and **old school gaming**, is the playing and collection of [personal computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_game "Computer game"), [consoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_console "Video game console"), and [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game "Video game") from earlier decades. Usually, retro gaming is based upon systems that are outmoded or discontinued, although ported retro gaming allows games to be played on modern hardware via [ports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porting "Porting"), [emulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_console_emulator "Video game console emulator") or compilations. It is typically for [nostalgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nostalgia "Nostalgia"), preservation, or authenticity. A new game could be retro styled, such as an [RPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role-playing_video_game "Role-playing video game") with turn-based combat and [pixel art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixel_art "Pixel art") in [isometric camera perspective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometric_video_game_graphics "Isometric video game graphics"), as well as [chip-tune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiptune "Chiptune") styled [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_music "Video game music").

Retro gaming has existed since the early years of the video game industry, and was popularized with the Internet and [emulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_console_emulator "Video game console emulator") technology. It is argued that the main reasons players are drawn to retro games are nostalgia for different eras, the idea that older games are more innovative and original, and the simplicity of the games.

Retro gaming and [retrocomputing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrocomputing "Retrocomputing") have been described as [preservation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_preservation "Digital preservation") activity and as aspects of the [remix culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remix_culture "Remix culture").
