| Metric Learning & Representation Learning Losses    | 2006–2024  | Learn meaningful feature embeddings by controlling distances or similarities        | Contrastive Loss, Triplet Loss, Center Loss, Lifted Structured Loss, N-pair Loss, Proxy-NCA, Proxy Anchor, Circle Loss, InfoNCE, NT-Xent, SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL, SimSiam, Barlow Twins, VICReg, DINO      | Face recognition, contrastive learning, self-supervised representation learning |

| Diffusion & Score-based Generative Losses           | 2005–2024  | Learn probability distributions through denoising or score estimation               | Score Matching Loss, Denoising Score Matching, DDPM Noise Prediction Loss, DDIM Objective, Score SDE Loss, EDM Objective, Consistency Model Loss, Flow Matching Loss, Rectified Flow Loss              | Score matching, diffusion models, continuous-time generative modeling           |
| Vision-specific Losses                              | 2007–2024  | Optimize dense prediction, segmentation, and detection tasks                        | Dice Loss, Generalized Dice Loss, Tversky Loss, Focal Tversky Loss, IoU Loss, GIoU Loss, DIoU Loss, CIoU Loss, Lovász Loss                                                                             | Medical segmentation, semantic segmentation, object detection                   |
| Sequence Modeling & NLP Losses                      | 2006–2024  | Optimize sequential prediction and language modeling                                | CTC Loss, Language Modeling Loss, Masked Language Modeling Loss, Seq2Seq Loss                                                                                                                          | Speech recognition, Transformers, large language models                         |
| LLM Alignment & Preference Optimization Losses      | 2022–2024  | Align language models with human preferences without or with reinforcement learning | Bradley-Terry Loss, Reward Modeling Loss, DPO Loss, IPO Loss, KTO Loss, ORPO Loss, SimPO Loss                                                                                                          | RLHF, preference optimization, instruction tuning                               |



| Loss function                       | Year | Category                                | Related literature (original citation)                                                                                                                                      | Mathematical expression                                                                                                                                                                                            | Brief introduction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ---: | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mean Absolute Error (MAE / L1 Loss) | 1757 | Regression                              | Laplace (1757), *Recherches sur plusieurs points d'analyse*                                                                                                                 | $L=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left\|y_i-\hat{y}_i\right\|_1$                                                                                                                                                        | A robust regression loss based on absolute deviations. It is less sensitive to outliers than squared error loss.                                                                                                                                         |
| Least Squares Loss (L2 Loss / MSE)  | 1805 | Regression                              | Legendre (1805), *Nouvelles méthodes pour la détermination des orbites des comètes*; Gauss (1809), *Theoria Motus Corporum Coelestium*                                      | $L=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2$                                                                                                                                                                     | The classical regression loss that minimizes squared prediction errors. Large errors are penalized more strongly.                                                                                                                                        |
| Maximum Likelihood Loss             | 1922 | Statistical Learning                    | Fisher (1922), *On the Mathematical Foundations of Theoretical Statistics*                                                                                                  | $L=-\sum_i\log p(y_i;\theta)$                                                                                                                                                                                      | Converts maximum likelihood estimation into an optimization objective by minimizing negative log likelihood.                                                                                                                                             |
| Cross Entropy Loss                  | 1948 | Classification                          | Shannon (1948), *A Mathematical Theory of Communication*                                                                                                                    | $L=-\sum_i y_i\log(p_i)$                                                                                                                                                                                           | Measures the difference between true and predicted probability distributions. It is the foundation of modern classification losses.                                                                                                                      |
| KL Divergence Loss                  | 1951 | Distribution Matching                   | Kullback & Leibler (1951), *On Information and Sufficiency*                                                                                                                 | $D_{KL}(P,Q)=\sum_xP(x)\log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}$                                                                                                                                                                      | Measures the difference between two probability distributions. Widely used in probabilistic modeling, VAEs, and knowledge distillation.                                                                                                                  |
| Perceptron Loss                     | 1958 | Classification                          | Rosenblatt (1958), *The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization in the Brain*                                                            | $L=\sum_i\max(0,-y_i(w^Tx_i+b))$                                                                                                                                                                                   | One of the earliest classification losses. Penalizes incorrectly classified samples.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Logistic Loss (Log Loss)            | 1958 | Classification                          | Cox (1958), *The Regression Analysis of Binary Sequences*                                                                                                                   | $L=-\frac{1}{N}\sum_i[y_i\log(\hat y_i)+(1-y_i)\log(1-\hat y_i)]$                                                                                                                                                  | The standard binary classification loss derived from logistic regression.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Huber Loss                          | 1964 | Regression / Robust Learning            | Huber (1964), *Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter*                                                                                                                   | $e=y-\hat y$ <br> $L_\delta(e)=0.5e^2,\ \text{if }\|e\|\leq\delta$ <br> $L_\delta(e)=\delta(\|e\|-0.5\delta),\ \text{otherwise}$                                                                                   | Combines MSE and MAE. It behaves like squared error for small errors and absolute error for large errors.                                                                                                                                                |
| Negative Log Likelihood (NLL Loss)  | 1972 | Classification / Probabilistic Modeling | Good (1972), *The Estimation of Probabilities: An Essay on Modern Bayesian Methods*                                                                                         | $L=-\sum_i\log p(y_i;\theta)$                                                                                                                                                                                      | A general probabilistic loss obtained from maximum likelihood estimation. It is commonly used for classification models.                                                                                                                                 |
| Quantile Loss (Pinball Loss)        | 1978 | Regression                              | Koenker & Bassett (1978), *Regression Quantiles*                                                                                                                            | $L_\tau(y,\hat y)=\max(\tau(y-\hat y),(\tau-1)(y-\hat y))$                                                                                                                                                         | Used for quantile regression. It predicts conditional quantiles rather than only conditional means.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Softmax Cross Entropy Loss          | 1986 | Classification                          | Bridle (1990), *Probabilistic Interpretation of Feedforward Classification Network Outputs*; Rumelhart et al. (1986), *Learning representations by back-propagating errors* | $p_i=\frac{e^{z_i}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}$ <br> $L=-\sum_i y_i\log(p_i)$                                                                                                                                                 | The standard multi-class classification loss used in deep neural networks.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Hinge Loss                          | 1995 | Classification                          | Vapnik (1995), *The Nature of Statistical Learning Theory*; Cortes & Vapnik (1995), *Support-vector networks*                                                               | $L=\sum_i\max(0,1-y_if(x_i))$                                                                                                                                                                                      | The loss function used by support vector machines. Encourages large classification margins.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Squared Hinge Loss                  | 1995 | Classification                          | Cortes & Vapnik (1995), *Support-vector networks*                                                                                                                           | $L=\sum_i\max(0,1-y_if(x_i))^2$                                                                                                                                                                                    | A smoother variant of hinge loss that increases the penalty for larger margin violations.                                                                                                                                                                |
| Smooth L1 Loss                      | 1998 | Regression / Object Detection           | Girshick (2015), *Fast R-CNN*                                                                                                                                               | $e=y-\hat y$ <br> $L(e)=0.5e^2,\ \text{if }\|e\|<1$ <br> $L(e)=\|e\|-0.5,\ \text{otherwise}$                                                                                                                       | A differentiable approximation of L1 loss. Widely used for bounding box regression.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Log-Cosh Loss                       | 2002 | Regression / Robust Learning            | Chen et al. (2002), *Robust Learning with Log-Cosh Loss*                                                                                                                    | $L=\sum_i\log(\cosh(y_i-\hat y_i))$                                                                                                                                                                                | A smooth robust regression loss. Behaves similarly to MSE near zero and MAE for large errors.                                                                                                                                                            |
| Contrastive Loss                    | 2006 | Metric Learning                         | Hadsell, Chopra & LeCun (2006), *Dimensionality Reduction by Learning an Invariant Mapping*                                                                                 | $L=yD^2+(1-y)max(0,m-D)^2$ <br> $D=dist(x_1,x_2)$                                                                                                                                                                  | Learns representations by pulling similar samples together and pushing dissimilar samples apart. The foundation of many metric learning methods.                                                                                                         |
| Triplet Loss                        | 2015 | Metric Learning                         | Schroff, Kalenichenko & Philbin (2015), *FaceNet: A Unified Embedding for Face Recognition and Clustering*                                                                  | $L=max(0,D(a,p)-D(a,n)+m)$                                                                                                                                                                                         | Learns embeddings using anchor, positive, and negative samples. Encourages the anchor-positive distance to be smaller than anchor-negative distance.                                                                                                     |
| Label Smoothing Loss                | 2016 | Classification / Regularization         | Szegedy et al. (2016), *Rethinking the Inception Architecture for Computer Vision*                                                                                          | $y_{smooth}=(1-\epsilon)y+\frac{\epsilon}{K}$ <br> $L=-\sum_i y_{smooth,i}\log(p_i)$                                                                                                                               | Regularizes classifiers by replacing hard labels with softened probability targets, reducing over-confidence.                                                                                                                                            |
| Lifted Structured Loss              | 2016 | Metric Learning                         | Song et al. (2016), *Deep Metric Learning via Lifted Structured Feature Embedding*                                                                                          | $L=\frac{1}{2}\sum_{i,j}max(0,m+D_{ij})^2$                                                                                                                                                                         | Uses all positive and negative pairs in a batch to create stronger metric learning supervision.                                                                                                                                                          |
| N-pair Loss                         | 2016 | Metric Learning                         | Sohn (2016), *Improved Deep Metric Learning with Multi-class N-pair Loss Objective*                                                                                         | $L=\log(1+\sum_j exp(x_a^Tx_j-x_a^Tx_p))$                                                                                                                                                                          | Generalizes triplet loss by comparing one positive example against multiple negatives simultaneously.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Focal Loss                          | 2017 | Classification / Imbalanced Learning    | Lin et al. (2017), *Focal Loss for Dense Object Detection*                                                                                                                  | $L=-\alpha(1-p_t)^\gamma\log(p_t)$                                                                                                                                                                                 | Modifies cross entropy by reducing the contribution of easy examples, improving learning under class imbalance.                                                                                                                                          |
| Center Loss                         | 2016 | Metric Learning / Classification        | Wen et al. (2016), *A Discriminative Feature Learning Approach for Deep Face Recognition*                                                                                   | $L_c=\frac{1}{2}\sum_i\|x_i-c_{y_i}\|^2$                                                                                                                                                                           | Encourages features from the same class to cluster around class centers, improving discriminative power.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Proxy-NCA Loss                      | 2017 | Metric Learning                         | Movshovitz-Attias et al. (2017), *No Fuss Distance Metric Learning using Proxies*                                                                                           | $L=-\log\frac{exp(-d(x_i,p_i))}{\sum_j exp(-d(x_i,p_j))}$                                                                                                                                                          | Replaces individual samples with learnable proxies, making metric learning more computationally efficient.                                                                                                                                               |
| InfoNCE Loss                        | 2018 | Representation Learning                 | Oord, Li & Vinyals (2018), *Representation Learning with Contrastive Predictive Coding*                                                                                     | $L=-log\frac{exp(sim(z_i,z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_k exp(sim(z_i,z_k)/\tau)}$                                                                                                                                               | A contrastive learning objective that maximizes agreement between positive pairs while separating negative samples.                                                                                                                                      |
| Proxy Anchor Loss                   | 2020 | Metric Learning                         | Kim et al. (2020), *Proxy Anchor Loss for Deep Metric Learning*                                                                                                             | $L=\frac{1}{\|\mathcal{P}\|}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}}\log(1+\sum_{x\in X_p^+}exp(-\alpha(s(x,p)-\delta)))$ <br> $+\frac{1}{\|\mathcal{P}\|}\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}}\log(1+\sum_{x\in X_p^-}exp(\alpha(s(x,p)+\delta)))$ | Uses learnable class proxies instead of individual sample pairs. Positive samples are pulled toward their proxies while negative samples are pushed away. It provides stable optimization and better scalability than pair-based metric learning losses. |
| Circle Loss                         | 2020 | Metric Learning                         | Sun et al. (2020), *Circle Loss: A Unified Perspective of Pair Similarity Optimization*                                                                                     | $L=log(1+\sum_p exp(\gamma\alpha_p(s_p-\Delta_p))\sum_n exp(\gamma\alpha_n(s_n-\Delta_n)))$                                                                                                                        | Unifies pair similarity optimization by assigning adaptive weights to positive and negative pairs.                                                                                                                                                       |
| SimCLR Loss (NT-Xent)               | 2020 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Chen et al. (2020), *A Simple Framework for Contrastive Learning of Visual Representations*                                                                                 | $L=-log\frac{exp(sim(z_i,z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_k exp(sim(z_i,z_k)/\tau)}$                                                                                                                                               | Contrastive loss used in SimCLR. Learns visual representations by maximizing agreement between augmented views.                                                                                                                                          |
| MoCo Loss                           | 2020 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | He et al. (2020), *Momentum Contrast for Unsupervised Visual Representation Learning*                                                                                       | $L=-log\frac{exp(q\cdot k^+/\tau)}{\sum_i exp(q\cdot k_i/\tau)}$                                                                                                                                                   | Uses a momentum encoder and memory queue to enable contrastive learning with many negative samples.                                                                                                                                                      |
| BYOL Loss                           | 2020 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Grill et al. (2020), *Bootstrap Your Own Latent: A New Approach to Self-Supervised Learning*                                                                                | $L=2-2\frac{q(z_\theta)\cdot z_\xi}{\|q(z_\theta)\|_2\|z_\xi\|_2}$                                                                                                                                                 | Learns representations without negative samples by predicting one augmented view from another.                                                                                                                                                           |
| SimSiam Loss                        | 2021 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Chen & He (2021), *Exploring Simple Siamese Representation Learning*                                                                                                        | $L=-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{p_1\cdot stopgrad(z_2)}{\|p_1\|\|z_2\|}+\frac{p_2\cdot stopgrad(z_1)}{\|p_2\|\|z_1\|})$                                                                                                      | A simple Siamese network approach that avoids negative pairs and uses stop-gradient to prevent collapse.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Barlow Twins Loss                   | 2021 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Zbontar et al. (2021), *Barlow Twins: Self-Supervised Learning via Redundancy Reduction*                                                                                    | $L=\sum_i(1-C_{ii})^2+\lambda\sum_i\sum_{j\neq i}C_{ij}^2$                                                                                                                                                         | Encourages invariant representations while reducing redundancy between feature dimensions.                                                                                                                                                               |
| VICReg Loss                         | 2022 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Bardes, Ponce & LeCun (2022), *VICReg: Variance-Invariance-Covariance Regularization for Self-Supervised Learning*                                                          | $L=\lambda L_{inv}+\mu L_{var}+\nu L_{cov}$                                                                                                                                                                        | Combines invariance, variance preservation, and covariance regularization to prevent representation collapse.                                                                                                                                            |
| DINO Loss                           | 2021 | Self-supervised Representation Learning | Caron et al. (2021), *Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers*                                                                                           | $L=-\sum_i p_t(x_i)\log p_s(x_i)$                                                                                                                                                                                  | Self-distillation loss where a student network learns from a teacher network without labels.                                                                                                                                                             |
